01 · The Problem Context
The Unseen Crisis: Why Long-Term Maternal Care Breaks Down
📉
The Post-Discharge Drop-Off Cliff
Managing maternal care requires 1,000+ days across pregnancy, institutional delivery, and 7 infant vaccine cohorts.

Yet, over 25–30% of mothers and infants drop out of scheduled milestones within months after hospital discharge.
📋
Administrative Burden & Isolation
Dropouts are rarely due to medical negligence—they stem from administrative friction and emotional isolation.

Caregivers struggle with fragile paper MCP cards, confusing clinic schedules, and lack peer circles in their neighborhood.
📱
The "Cold Clinical App" Failure Mode
Conventional healthcare apps suffer 80%+ abandonment in 30 days because they are cold and transactional—opening only when an alert fires.

Without daily cultural rituals and peer community, patients and caregivers disengage entirely.

06 · Safety & Human Oversight
Clinical Safety: Zero Diagnostic Risk & Guardrail Moderation

Strict Non-Clinical Scope

  • ❌
    0% Diagnostic Advice: Never provides medical risk scores, diagnostic labels, or lab test interpretations.
  • ❌
    0% Treatment Prescriptions: Never suggests medications, dosage changes, or clinical therapies.
  • ✅
    Assistive Only: Limited strictly to administrative scheduling, nutrition reminders, non-strenuous lifestyle, and welfare schemes.

NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails

  • 🛡️
    In-Memory Guardrails: Every user prompt, AI response, and community post passes through semantic filters.
  • 🚨
    Symptom Interception: If clinical symptoms (bleeding, severe pain, fever) are mentioned, automation immediately halts.
  • 📞
    Emergency Routing: Instantly connects to the nearest PHC emergency desk and national ambulance helpline.

Human-in-the-Loop & Audit

  • 👩‍⚕️
    Clinician & ANM Oversight: Frontline staff review milestone rosters and due-lists with 1-click approvals.
  • 📜
    Immutable PostgreSQL Audit Log: Every AI recommendation, user confirmation, and override is permanently logged.
  • 🔒
    Caregiver Consent: Family synchronization requires explicit patient opt-in and consent management.
