01 · The Problem Context
The Unseen Crisis: Why Long-Term Maternal Care Breaks Down
📉
Post-Discharge Drop-Off Cliff
Managing maternal health requires 1,000+ continuous days across pregnancy, institutional delivery, and 7 infant vaccine cohorts.
• 30% Drop-Out: Mothers disengage after hospital discharge due to zero daily touchpoints.
• Late Mortality Risk: Over 50% of maternal complications occur at home during the 42-day postpartum period.
• Vaccine Gaps: Severe immunization drop between the 14-week and 9-month doses.
Primary Vulnerability: Continuity Cliff
📋
Administrative Friction & Isolation
Caregiver dropouts stem from logistical overload, fragile paper records, and lack of nearby peer empathy circles.
• Fragile Paper MCP Cards: Easily misplaced, stained, or damaged across seasonal migrations.
• Logistical Overload: 18+ separate clinical visits, lab tests, ultrasound scans, and welfare schemes.
• Family Disconnect: Spouses and elders are completely excluded from active care synchronization.
Primary Friction: Paperwork & Logistics
📱
The "Cold Clinical App" Failure
Standard healthcare apps suffer 85%+ abandonment within 30 days because they are cold and transactional.
• Zero Daily Pull: Mothers never open an app solely to view a monthly appointment reminder.
• English & Jargon: Complex clinical terms alienate vernacular and rural families.
• Culturally Disconnected: Missing daily bonding, Garbh Sanskar rituals, and peer community warmth.
Primary Hurdle: Habit Disconnection

Postgres 16 pgvector RLS Audit Ledger

06 · Safety & Human Oversight
Clinical Safety: Zero Diagnostic Risk & Guardrail Moderation

Strict Non-Clinical Scope

Mandatory Boundary
  • ❌
    0% Diagnostic Advice: Never infers medical diseases, pregnancy risk scores, or clinical conditions.
  • ❌
    0% Prescriptions: Strictly prohibits suggesting pharmaceutical drugs, doses, or medication edits.
  • ❌
    0% Lab Interpretation: Never attempts to interpret ultrasound Doppler scans or blood work numbers.
  • ✅
    Operational Assistive Only: Strictly constrained to scheduling, diet charts, and welfare schemes.
  • 📋
    Persistent Disclaimers: Clear advisory banner visible on every interaction and notification.
🛡️ Zero Clinical Liability · Strictly Non-Diagnostic

NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails

Automated AI Defense
  • 🛡️
    In-Memory Semantic Firewall: Dual-pass validation on both user prompts and model responses.
  • 🚨
    Red-Flag Interception: Traps critical keywords (bleeding, acute pain, high fever, fluid loss).
  • 🛑
    Instant AI Halt: Freezes non-clinical chat when an acute medical emergency is suspected.
  • 📞
    108 / PHC Fast-Routing: Immediately displays 1-tap emergency dialer with PHC GPS coordinates.
  • 🚫
    Misinformation Filter: Blocks harmful folk remedies and unverified claims in Birth Clubs.
🤖 Real-Time Semantic Interception Powered by Colang
