The 1,000-Day Solution

In traditional systems, 68% of maternal engagement terminates within 6 weeks post-delivery. Swasth replaces clinical drop-off with a continuous cultural bridge:

1
Antenatal Continuity: TT-1 & TT-2 vaccinations, NT ultrasound, iron/calcium supplements.
2
Pediatric Milestone Engine: Automated tracking for 6, 10, and 14-week immunization cohorts (Pentavalent, Rota, IPV).
3
Weaning Diet Guidance: Month 6 transition to ragi malt, dal water, and steamed fruits paired with peer birth clubs.
