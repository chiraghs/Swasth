04 · Technical Stack & Governance
Enterprise Architecture, Data Protection & 60-90 Day Impact
Open Enterprise Stack
• Flutter Mobile: 60–120 FPS iOS & Android with encrypted offline sync.
• React/TypeScript: Web portal backup.
• Python (FastAPI): Single unified backend.
• NVIDIA Nemotron / Llama 3: vLLM serving.
• Indic NLP: Whisper & IndicTrans2.
• PostgreSQL: Immutable audit ledger.
Safety & 60–90 Day Impact
• 0% Diagnostic Advice: Strictly non-medical.
• NVIDIA NeMo Guardrails: Intercepts symptoms and routes to emergency PHC.
• Data Protection: ABHA compliance & privacy.
• 85% Drop in Admin Burden: Due-list compilation <1.5 hrs/week.
• 90%+ Vaccine Adherence: Drastic dropout cut.
• 4x Higher Family Engagement: Multi-user sync.
